### Added

- **`empty` and `list` see what Finder cannot.** macOS hides AppleDouble `._` metadata files in the Trash from Finder and from every app built on Foundation: a Trash can hold thousands of them while Finder shows it empty and greys out Empty Trash, leaving the bytes on disk indefinitely. `trash list` now reports them ("+ N hidden `._` metadata files invisible to Finder"), and `trash empty` deletes them with the rest, stating the count in its confirmation. Empty means empty.
